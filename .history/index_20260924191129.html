OUR APPROACH

The Right Grant.The Right Story.The Right Strategy.

Successful grant funding starts long before the application and continues after the award. Our approach brings research, strategy, writing, and grant management together into one clear process, so every opportunity you pursue has purpose behind it.

DISCOVER

We Start With Your Mission.

Before we search for funding, we take the time to understand your organization, programs, goals, community, and funding priorities. This gives us the foundation to identify opportunities that truly align with the work you do.

Understand the mission. Define the need. Set the direction.
RESEARCH

We Find the Right Opportunities.

More grants don't always mean more funding. We research and qualify opportunities based on eligibility, alignment, funding priorities, deadlines, and your likelihood of being a strong candidate.

Less chasing. More strategic opportunities.
DEVELOP

We Build a Compelling Case.

We turn your programs, impact, data, and vision into a clear proposal tailored to each funder. Every application is developed to communicate not only what you need, but why your work deserves investment.

Clear narratives. Strong applications. Funder-focused storytelling.
SUPPORT

We Stay With You Beyond Submission.

Our work doesn't have to end when you hit submit. We can help manage your grant pipeline, reporting requirements, renewals, deadlines, and future opportunities, giving you a more consistent approach to funding.

From first opportunity to long-term funding strategy.
About

Helping Purpose-Driven Organizations Secure the Funding They Deserve.

Yusuff Azeez Olanrewaju is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Hope Grant and Development Consults Limited, a grants and fundraising consulting firm supporting nonprofits, healthcare organisations, and social impact ventures in Nigeria and the United States to secure funding, strengthen resource mobilisation strategies, and build sustainable programme infrastructure.

A pharmacist by training and a public health professional by practice, Yusuff holds a Bachelor of Pharmacy from the University of Ibadan, where he graduated as the best student in Pharmacology, Therapeutics and Toxicology, receiving the Board of Fellows Award from the Pharmaceutical Society of Nigeria. He was also recognised by Pharmanews Limited as one of the Top Five Pharmacy Students in Nigeria promoting public health activities and research. He is currently completing a Master of Public Health in Disaster and Humanitarian Management at Iconic Open University, Abuja.
